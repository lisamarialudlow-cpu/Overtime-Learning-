About Overtime Learning

Overtime Learning is a fictional course-matching service for parents in Navan, County Meath, who want to return to education around school hours.

The problem

Parents may need to check individual timetables before finding courses that suit their family responsibilities.

Our solution

Select your free days, set your hours, choose interests and receive automatically calculated course matches with explanations for conflicts.

Six customer requirements

  1. Select available weekdays.
  2. Set available hours and travel allowance.
  3. Filter by subject interest.
  4. Automatically identify matching courses.
  5. Explain timetable conflicts.
  6. Save favourites and preferences after refresh.

Existing alternatives

FETCH Courses and LMETB's course finder provide existing course-search information. Overtime Learning explores a more personalised school-hours matching experience.

Alternatives reviewed: 25 October 2026.

How the service could be funded

The proposed €5 monthly subscription could support future course updates, hosting and maintenance. Revenue depends on actual paying subscribers. The price and business model are assumptions.

Prototype status

Course matching, preferences, favourites and sample news matching are demonstrated locally in the browser. Payments, live listings and real notifications are planned only.
