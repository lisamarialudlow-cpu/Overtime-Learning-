THE PROBLEM

Going back to education shouldn't mean missing the school run.

For parents in Navan returning to part-time education, finding a suitable course can involve checking individual timetables and comparing them with family commitments.

"I'd love to go back to education, but I need to collect my children from school. How do I find a course that fits?"

Invented example quote illustrating the proposed customer's problem.

1

Limited availability

Parents may only be available during particular weekdays and school hours.

2

Timetable conflicts

Courses may begin too early or finish too late for school collection.

3

Time-consuming searches

Comparing course timetables can take valuable time away from busy parents.

Our customer: Parents living in Navan, County Meath, who want to return to part-time education but need to work around school collection. Parents use the service, choose their own courses and would be the proposed paying subscribers.

THE SOLUTION

Three simple steps to finding your fit.

Overtime Learning is a mobile-first educational course-matching tool designed around busy parents.

1

Choose your free time

Select the days you're available, choose your hours and allow time for school collection.

2

Find your fit

See matching courses, understand timetable conflicts and save your favourites.

INDEPENDENT USER TESTING

Tested, reviewed and improved.

The prototype was tested independently, and feedback was provided by email on 26 October 2026.

What the tester found

The tester explored the webpage and reported that the main functions appeared to work.

However, they felt that the original dark-green background was too dark.

They also found it difficult to access saved courses that did not match their timetable.

What we changed

The background was updated to a brighter emerald-green colour scheme.

A dedicated My Saved Courses screen and heart-shaped favourites button were introduced.

Parents can now revisit courses they have saved, including those that do not match their timetable.

Testing summary based on actual feedback. No tester contact information is published.
