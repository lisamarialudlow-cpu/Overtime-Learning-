About Overtime Learning

Overtime Learning is a fictional mobile-first course matching service for parents in Navan, County Meath, who want to return to part-time education.

The Problem

Parents may spend time checking different course timetables before finding learning opportunities that fit around the school run.

How It Works

Select your available weekdays, choose your hours, pick your interests and receive automatically calculated course matches.

Our Six Requirements

  1. Select available weekdays.
  2. Set available hours and travel allowance.
  3. Filter by course interest.
  4. Automatically identify matching courses.
  5. Explain unsuitable timetables.
  6. Save favourites and preferences after refresh.

Existing Alternatives

FETCH Courses and LMETB's course finder provide existing education and training information. Overtime Learning explores personalised matching around school-hour availability.

Alternatives reviewed: 25 October 2026.

Business Model

A proposed €5 monthly subscription could help sustain future course news, updates, hosting and maintenance. This is an assumed funding model, not actual revenue.

Prototype Status

Course matching, saved preferences, favourites and sample news matching are demonstrated. Live listings, payment processing and real notifications are planned only.
